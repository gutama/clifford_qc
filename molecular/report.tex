\noindent\textbf{Reading the table.} A-CASE reaches chemical accuracy on 5 of 7 geometries. CCSD has a smaller absolute error on 7 of 7 geometries. Negative CCSD errors are possible because coupled cluster is not variational. Some capped stretched runs miss chemical accuracy; inspect their errors and spin contamination alongside basis size.

\noindent\textbf{The stopping rule is oracle-assisted.} Where enabled, growth may halt when the error against the \emph{exact} energy falls below threshold. Runs may also terminate at a budget or another stopping condition. The $M$ column is the selected basis size, not a guarantee that the accuracy target was reached.

\noindent$^\dagger$ The complete SD basis spans the entire sector for this molecule, so agreement with exact diagonalization is arithmetic rather than accuracy.
